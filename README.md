1. Explain Minimax in your own words.
Minimax is a decision-making algorithm that is used in a two-player games such as this Tic-Tac-Toe game. It works by looking at all the possible moves a player can make and predicting how they will respond. The idea is that one player tries to maximize their score (the best possible outcome), while the other tries to minimize it (make the opponent’s outcome as bad as possible). Minimax helps computers to think ahead, choosing the move that gives the best chance of winning if both players play perfectly.
